# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Materials researchers reviewing electron diffraction images against CIF files,
then preparing annotated figures for papers on a Windows desktop.

## Product Purpose
Keep measured evidence, provisional indexing and publication styling distinct.
Make calibration and detection adjustable, inspectable and reversible.

## Brand Personality
Precise, quiet, practical. The user selected a dark image-workbench interface.

## Anti-references
Avoid long undifferentiated option lists, decorative dashboards and marketing
layouts. Never let visual styling imply that a scientific candidate is confirmed.

## Design Principles
- Keep the image central and scientific controls close to their results.
- Group related settings in compact rounded tool sections.
- Separate calibration, detection, display and publication workflows.
- Preserve manual correction, undo and clean export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Retain native Qt keyboard controls, readable labels and icon tooltips. Use
high-contrast neutral surfaces; do not rely on color alone for scientific state.
